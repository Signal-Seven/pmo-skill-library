## Scoring Method
Score each initiative's contribution to each OKR:
- 3 = Primary driver of this OKR's key results
- 2 = Meaningful contributor to this OKR
- 1 = Indirect or secondary contribution
- 0 = No contribution to this OKR

---

## Alert Thresholds [CUSTOMIZE]
- Portfolio contribution below [15] per OKR: flag for leadership
- Active initiative with total OKR score of [2] or below: misalignment flag

## Analysis Output

**Per Initiative:** OKR score matrix (one column per OKR), total score
**Per OKR:** Sum of all active initiative scores vs. alert threshold, status (Met / At Risk)

**Flagged items:**
- OKRs below threshold: list with top intake queue candidates that address the gap
- Low-alignment actives: initiatives contributing to no current goal

**Executive Summary:**
Two sentences on portfolio alignment health. One sentence on highest-priority gap.
